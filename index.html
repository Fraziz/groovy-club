Philosophy

Recognized by Fit and Feel

Groovy Club was born from a simple conviction: clothing should speak first through its silhouette, weight, and construction before loud logos ever enter the room.

We call our design language Vintage Street Formal. It brings together the nostalgic character and relaxed proportions of vintage sportswear with the sharp discipline and longevity of classic tailoring.

Every garment is developed around substantial fabric weights—starting at 260 GSM compact cotton—and finished with our signature warm camel print color #CEA370. We believe in clothing people reach for every morning with instinctive confidence.

Craftsmanship

The Considered Details

A closer look at the intentional elements that define every Groovy Club piece.

Signature Line

The Groovy Curve

Our proprietary curved cut line appears in selected pocket shapes, yoke seams, and hemlines. Inspired by mid-century tailoring arches, it provides an ergonomic flow that breaks away from rigid streetwear boxiness.

Structural Integrity

Thick Structured Collar & 260 GSM

A high-density 1.25" rib crewneck built with tight tension to retain its crisp circular shape after daily wear. Paired with compact combed cotton that resists fuzzing and maintains a clean matte surface.

◆

Controlled Dropped Shoulders

Shoulder seams dropped exactly enough to create a relaxed silhouette without excess bulk under outerwear.

◆

Matte Water-Based Inks

Screen-printed exclusively in signature #CEA370, allowing the garment's natural hand feel to shine through.

◆

Single Pleat Architecture

Trousers cut with deep forward single pleats that create an elegant vertical crease and generous drape in motion.
